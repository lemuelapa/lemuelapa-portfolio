CONTEXT & OWNERSHIP

A long-term role.
Hands-on execution.

Since July 2022, I’ve worked at XO3D as its sole developer. As Head of Web Development, I combine technical ownership with day-to-day implementation across its website and public digital experiences.

The contributions below focus on the documented June–October 2026 work: the Astro rebuild, content migration, technical SEO, performance, accessibility, and conversion improvements.

The website moved from WordPress and Elementor to Astro on Cloudflare Pages, with bespoke templates and interactive content built around the studio’s services and portfolio.

01 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Website rebuild & content migration

From WordPress to an Astro site built around the studio’s work.

Platform migration
Migrated xo3d.co.uk from WordPress and Elementor to Astro, launched on Cloudflare Pages, and completed post-launch hardening. Subsequently upgraded Astro from version 5 to 7.
Service architecture
Built eight bespoke service pages covering product rendering, product animation, launch visuals, product CGI films, CGI solutions, product AR, product interactives, and product modelling. Updated the shared services template to carry the same design standard.
Case studies
Built the case studies hub and 30 bespoke project pages, including Nanuskate, Petalite, Roest, Sony, and AUAR.
Publishing & resources
Built the blog hub with search and pagination and migrated 207 posts. Migrated 57 resource pages, then rebuilt the 55 live resources on the launch-visuals template with a table of contents, author block, and FAQs.
Site-wide content systems
Built seven Tier 1 use-case pages alongside industries, guides, and thinking pages. Standardised hero sections across 35 pages and expanded the hubs to full width.
Migration quality assurance
Delivered the Phase 1 migration QA pack, image audits, and media-parity checks against staging. Delivered 50 images across 34 pages and moved code repositories into the XO3D GitHub organisation.
02 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Search visibility & structured data

Technical SEO and clearer entity information for search and AI discovery.

Technical cleanup
Resolved 1,345 structured-data errors, 43 broken images, duplicate H1s, link-anchor issues, and internal-link gaps across 63 under-linked pages identified in Semrush.
Search snippets
Completed a 53-page search-snippet rewrite campaign and submitted indexing requests. Normalised title suffixes and shortened 35 long meta descriptions.
Sitemaps & schema
Consolidated sitemaps, added a video sitemap and VideoObject schema, configured hreflang, and rewrote llms.txt.
Consistent dates & entities
Aligned visible dates, structured-data dates, and sitemap lastmod across approximately 309 pages. Added a canonical entity block to 136 blog posts and service pages, and clarified the XO3D entity in the case-study introduction.
Redirects & internal links
Built redirects for legacy, retired, and Labs tool URLs. Corrected in-body links pointing to retired pages and repaired links affected by email obfuscation.
Outreach preparation
Researched a 56-target backlink list and a separate 35-target link-and-mention list with draft outreach requests. These were research deliverables, not a claim of acquired backlinks.
03 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Performance & accessibility

Faster loading and more usable navigation across the site.

Homepage performance
Raised the mobile Lighthouse score from 48 to approximately 95 and reduced Largest Contentful Paint from 9.6 seconds to 2.1 seconds in the recorded before-and-after checks.
Image loading
Applied an LCP preload sweep across 68 pages, added responsive image srcset support and explicit image dimensions, and self-hosted WebP assets.
Rendering & assets
Self-hosted the Aileron font and favicon, resolved a forced-reflow issue, and adjusted reveal animations so content visible on initial load is not unnecessarily faded in.
Page-level checks
Ran PageSpeed optimisation passes on product interactives and the AUAR and Nanuskate case studies.
Accessible interactions
Completed two rounds of accessibility audits, repaired keyboard focus and the navigation menu’s inert state, and corrected the AUAR tablist with focus and ARIA improvements.
04 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Design systems & interactive experiences

Consistent presentation for case studies, 3D content, and product exploration.

Case-study system
Applied the AUAR case-study design standard across all 30 case studies, including grid layouts, reveal behaviour, aspect ratios, mobile fixes, and a featured-six treatment.
Reusable components
Rebuilt the CaseStudyAnswer component, LabsToolCTA variants, and service-page ordinal styling.
Visual consistency
Converted all-caps headings to Title Case, removed em dashes across 239 files, removed rounded corners from work media, and sharpened the industries and use-case hero designs.
AR & configurators
Rebuilt the Masterclip and Fitbit AR viewers. Added the Acumen furniture configurator, including a mobile scroll fix and options toggle, and added an AR table embed to the furniture-rendering page.
Viewer reliability
Added loading spinners to 3D viewers and AR embeds, integrated the Pandani jewellery viewer and optimised its model, and replaced a broken 3D/AR embed with a working AR viewer.
05 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Enquiries, analytics & integrations

A clearer enquiry journey, with working integrations and measurement.

Contact form rebuild

Marketing measurement

Social sharing

Business integrations
