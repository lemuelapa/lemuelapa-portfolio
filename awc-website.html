CONTEXT & COLLABORATION

Design supplied.
Developed by me.

All Weather Coating is an XO3D client. Since July 2022, I’ve worked on its website as a Full-Stack WordPress Developer and Elementor Specialist, with ongoing maintenance continuing today.

I built the website from scratch and implemented its redesign as the sole developer. A coworker provided the visual design through Figma; I handled the development and implementation.

My contribution also covers migration from WP Engine to SiteGround, SEO, performance, security, the contact form, and the visualiser tool. The reported outcome is a faster, more stable website.
